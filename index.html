Why this exists

"Human oversight" is doing a lot of unexamined work.

UK AI policy leans heavily on cross-sectoral language like "meaningful human review" or "appropriate safeguards," and leaves the specifics — who reviews, when, and with what authority — to be filled in ad hoc, sector by sector.

That ambiguity is invisible on paper. A clause can read as responsible in a policy document while still letting a real incident slip past every checkpoint it was meant to catch. This tool exists to surface that gap before an incident does.
