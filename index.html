WHY THIS EXISTS

The gap between regulatory language and practical implementation

Most oversight definitions sound plausible in a document. They name values, invoke principles, and gesture toward accountability. But when an AI system behaves unexpectedly, definitions are stress-tested in ways their authors never imagined.

Signal Check asks four concrete questions of any definition: Who is the human in the loop? When do they look? How would they know something is wrong? And can they actually do anything about it?
